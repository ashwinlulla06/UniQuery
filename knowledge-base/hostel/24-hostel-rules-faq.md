# Hostel Rules FAQ

This FAQ summarises the main residential rules for students living in Northbridge Institute of Technology hostels. Residents remain responsible for the complete residential agreement, safety instructions and official notices issued during the semester.

## Who may live in an Institute hostel?

Only a student with a current hostel allotment may occupy the assigned room. The allotment applies to the named student, room and semester. It cannot be transferred, exchanged or sublet without written approval from the Student Housing Office.

Visitors, friends and students on the waiting list must not stay overnight in a hostel room.

## What must I present at check-in?

A student must present the final allotment notice, Institute ID or admission letter, hostel payment acknowledgement and one recent passport-size photograph. The student must also complete the room inventory and provide current guardian and emergency-contact details.

Any existing damage or missing item should be recorded on the inventory before the room is accepted.

## When are hostel and mess payments due?

The Autumn 2026 hostel allocation circular states that a student offered a room must complete payment by 5:00 p.m. on 1 August 2026. A later offer made from the waiting list will carry its own payment deadline.

The notified semester charges are INR 28,000 for the hostel room and INR 24,000 as the mess advance. A hostel caution deposit of INR 5,000 is payable at first allotment. The applicable fee structure and residential agreement govern collection, adjustment and refund.

## Can I choose or change my room?

Students may state a room preference during application, but a preference is not guaranteed. Allocation depends on eligibility, room category, approved accommodation needs and available capacity.

A room change requires written approval. Students must not exchange keys, move belongings into another room or occupy a vacant bed while a request is pending.

## Can another person use my room?

No. A room or bed may be used only by the student to whom it is allotted. Lending a room key or access credential, allowing unauthorised overnight occupancy, or collecting payment for room access is prohibited.

## What are the visitor rules?

Visitors may meet residents only in designated common areas during notified visiting hours. The resident is responsible for the visitor's conduct and must follow the entry-register and identification procedure.

The Warden may restrict visitors during examinations, emergencies, maintenance work or another period requiring additional security. Overnight visitors are not permitted unless the Student Housing Office has issued specific written approval for an official purpose.

## Are there hostel entry timings?

Residents must follow the entry and quiet-hour timings displayed by the hostel. A student expecting to return after the notified entry time should use the late-entry procedure and provide the required reason and contact information.

Repeated unexplained late entry, false entries or bypassing access controls may be referred to the Warden. Entry restrictions do not prevent emergency access or approved academic, medical or placement-related movement.

## What are the quiet hours?

Quiet hours normally apply from 10:30 p.m. to 6:00 a.m. Residents must keep conversations, calls, music and electronic devices at a level that cannot be heard outside their room during this period.

Noise that disrupts study, sleep or official activity is not acceptable at any time. Common rooms may carry separate hours displayed at the entrance.

## May I cook in my room?

Cooking in student rooms is prohibited. Heating coils, hot plates, gas appliances and other exposed heating devices must not be kept or used in a room.

Only electrical items expressly permitted by the hostel may be used. Approved kettles or similar low-power appliances, where allowed, must be operated safely and disconnected after use. The Warden may remove an unsafe or prohibited appliance.

## Are smoking and alcohol permitted?

Smoking, vaping, alcohol, illegal drugs and possession of related prohibited material are not permitted in hostel buildings or other areas covered by Institute policy. A suspected medical emergency should be reported immediately rather than concealed because of possible disciplinary action.

## How should rooms and common areas be maintained?

Residents must keep their rooms reasonably clean and use waste-disposal points correctly. Corridors, stairways, exits and fire equipment must remain unobstructed.

Furniture must not be removed, dismantled or transferred between rooms. Nails, permanent adhesive, paint or another alteration that damages Institute property may lead to restoration charges.

## When may staff enter a room?

Authorised staff may enter for scheduled inspection, maintenance, safety checks, welfare concerns or an emergency. Advance notice will normally be given for routine work. Immediate access may be required when there is a fire alarm, water leak, electrical hazard, medical concern or reasonable belief of a serious rule violation.

## What should I do if something needs repair?

Submit a maintenance request through the hostel service portal and include the room number, problem and urgency. Water leaks, exposed wiring, smoke, a damaged lock or another immediate hazard must also be reported to the duty supervisor without delay.

Students must not arrange structural, electrical or plumbing repairs independently.

## Who is responsible for loss or damage?

Residents are responsible for reasonable care of their rooms, keys and issued property. The cost of damage beyond ordinary wear may be charged to the responsible student. Shared-area damage may be investigated before responsibility is assigned.

Students should secure money, documents and electronic devices. The Institute does not accept responsibility for unattended personal property except where liability is established under applicable law.

## What if I lose my room key or access card?

Report the loss immediately to the hostel office or duty supervisor. A temporary access arrangement may be made after identity is verified. Replacement and lock-change charges may apply.

A lost student ID card must also be reported through Student ID Card Services so its electronic access can be blocked.

## Can I remain in the hostel during a vacation?

Hostel accommodation does not automatically continue through a vacation or semester break. Students requiring approved vacation residence must apply by the announced deadline and provide the academic, internship, travel or other supporting reason requested by the Student Housing Office.

Some buildings may close or operate with reduced services during a break, and residents may be moved to designated rooms.

## How do I withdraw from hostel accommodation?

A resident must submit the prescribed cancellation request, clear room inventory and key obligations, and complete the check-out procedure. Simply leaving the room or handing a key to another student does not close the hostel account.

Any refund or deduction is determined under the applicable fee policy and residential agreement. The check-out record should be retained until the student's account is updated.

## What happens if hostel rules are broken?

The Warden may issue an advisory, require corrective action or refer the matter for disciplinary review, depending on its seriousness and frequency. Serious safety, violence, harassment, unauthorised occupancy, substance or property-related matters may lead to immediate protective measures while they are investigated.

A student will ordinarily be informed of the allegation and given an opportunity to respond under the applicable Institute procedure.

## How do I report an emergency or safety concern?

For an immediate threat to life or property, contact campus security at extension 2000 and alert the duty supervisor. Fire alarms must be treated as genuine, and residents must use the marked evacuation route without waiting to collect belongings.

Non-emergency residential concerns may be reported to `housing.office@northbridge.example`. Reports should identify the hostel, room or location and provide enough information for follow-up.
