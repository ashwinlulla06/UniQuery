PURPOSE

This directory lists the main offices that handle student enquiries. Students should contact the office responsible for the matter and include their student number and any existing application, payment or service reference.

Do not send passwords, one-time passwords, complete bank details, medical records or full identity-document numbers through ordinary email. The Institute may ask a student to present sensitive records through the portal or in person.

GENERAL CONTACT RULES

1. Use the Institute email account whenever possible.
2. Write a specific subject line and identify the service required.
3. Include the student number and relevant reference, but do not include unnecessary sensitive information.
4. Allow the published processing period before sending a repeat request.
5. Keep the acknowledgement or ticket number until the matter is closed.
6. For an immediate threat to life or property, contact Campus Security instead of waiting for an email response.
