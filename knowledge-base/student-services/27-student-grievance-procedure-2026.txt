STUDENT GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE 2026

Issued by: Office of the Registrar
Published: 25 July 2026
Effective from: 1 August 2026
Version: 1.0

1. PURPOSE

This procedure provides a clear route for a student to raise a concern about an Institute service, administrative decision or failure to follow a published process. It is intended to support fair review, timely communication and a written outcome.

The procedure does not guarantee the result requested by the student. It ensures that the concern is sent to an appropriate office and considered under the applicable rules.

2. MATTERS COVERED

A grievance may concern an unreasonable delay, incorrect application of a published procedure, failure to provide an approved service, inaccurate administrative record, unfair treatment in an administrative process or another service-related action within the Institute's responsibility.

Examples include an unresolved registration record, failure to process a complete application within the published period, inconsistent application of a hostel procedure or an administrative decision that does not identify its policy basis.

3. MATTERS HANDLED UNDER OTHER PROCEDURES

The following matters should normally use their specialised procedure:

Academic grade disagreement or re-evaluation: Controller of Examinations procedure.
Attendance record correction: department verification and Academic Affairs process.
Disciplinary allegation or appeal: student discipline regulations.
Harassment or discrimination: equal opportunity and safeguarding procedure.
Scholarship selection review: scholarship policy and notified review route.
Employment complaint by a student employee: applicable employment procedure.
Immediate safety incident or suspected crime: Campus Security.

The Student Grievance Office will redirect a submission when another procedure clearly applies. Redirection is not a decision on the merits of the concern.

4. INFORMAL RESOLUTION

Where safe and reasonable, the student should first contact the office that provided the service or made the decision. The student should explain the problem, identify the requested correction and retain the correspondence or service reference.

The responsible office should normally reply within five working days. An informal enquiry is not required where the concern involves possible retaliation, a serious conflict of interest, sensitive misconduct or an immediate deadline that would make informal resolution ineffective.

5. FORMAL SUBMISSION

A formal grievance must be submitted through the student grievance portal using Form GRV-01. If the portal is unavailable, the Student Grievance Office may provide an accessible alternative.

The submission must include:

Student name and student number.
Institute email address and current programme.
The office, decision, service or event concerned.
Relevant dates and existing application or ticket references.
A clear description of what occurred.
Steps already taken to resolve the matter.
The outcome or correction requested.
Copies of relevant notices, correspondence or records.

Passwords, complete payment-card details and unrelated personal records must not be included.

6. SUBMISSION PERIOD

A grievance should be submitted within 20 working days after the student became aware of the matter. A later submission may be accepted when illness, delayed access to records, a continuing problem or another reasonable circumstance explains the delay.

The student should explain the reason for delay. Acceptance of a late grievance does not imply that the grievance will be upheld.

7. ACKNOWLEDGEMENT AND INITIAL REVIEW

The Student Grievance Office will normally acknowledge a formal submission within two working days and issue a case reference in the format GRV-2026-XXXXX.

The initial review checks whether the submission is understandable, within Institute responsibility and supported by enough information to begin review. The office may request missing records or redirect the case to a specialised procedure.

If additional information is requested, the student should provide it within five working days unless an extension is agreed. A case may be closed when essential information is not provided after a reminder.

8. REVIEW OFFICER

An officer who was not directly responsible for the disputed action will normally conduct the review. The officer may examine records, request a response from the responsible office and speak with the student or relevant employees.

The review officer must disclose a conflict of interest. When a reasonable conflict exists, the case will be reassigned.

9. INTERIM ACTION

Submitting a grievance does not automatically suspend a deadline, fee, examination decision, hostel action or other requirement. The student must request interim action separately and explain why it is necessary to prevent significant and irreversible harm.

The authorised office may approve, refuse or limit interim action. An interim arrangement does not determine the final outcome.

10. EXPECTED REVIEW PERIOD

A written outcome should normally be issued within 15 working days after the complete grievance is accepted for review. Complex cases may require additional time.

If the review cannot be completed within that period, the Student Grievance Office should inform the student of the reason and provide a revised expected date. Time spent waiting for information requested from the student may be excluded from the review period.

11. POSSIBLE OUTCOMES

The written outcome may:

Uphold the grievance in full or in part.
Decline the grievance with reasons.
Correct an administrative record.
Require an office to reconsider a decision under the correct procedure.
Provide a delayed service by a stated date.
Recommend a process improvement.
Refer a separate concern to another authorised procedure.

The outcome should identify the information considered, the applicable rule or process and any action to be taken. The grievance process cannot award a qualification, alter an academic grade outside the examination procedure or create an exception that the Institute has no authority to grant.

12. REQUEST FOR REVIEW

A student may request one review of the formal outcome within ten working days of receiving it. The request must use Form GRV-02 and identify at least one of the following grounds:

A material procedural error affected the outcome.
Relevant evidence was available but was not reasonably considered.
New evidence could not reasonably have been provided earlier.
The stated outcome is inconsistent with the policy identified in the decision.

Disagreement with the outcome alone is not a ground for review. The review officer may confirm the outcome, modify it or return the matter for reconsideration. The review decision completes the internal grievance procedure unless another regulation expressly provides a further route.

13. SUPPORT AND REPRESENTATION

A student may seek assistance from an approved student adviser or accessibility support person. The student remains responsible for the accuracy of the submission and should participate directly unless an approved accommodation provides otherwise.

The Institute may communicate with a representative only after receiving the student's written authorisation and verifying the representative's identity.

14. CONFIDENTIALITY AND RECORDS

Grievance information is shared only with people who need it to assess, respond to or administer the case, subject to law and Institute policy. Complete confidentiality cannot be promised when investigation or safety action requires disclosure.

Case records are retained under the Institute records schedule. Anonymous information may be used to identify recurring service problems.

15. PROTECTION FROM RETALIATION

A student must not be disadvantaged for raising a concern in good faith or participating honestly in a review. A report of suspected retaliation should quote the original case reference and be sent promptly to the Student Grievance Office.

Knowingly false statements, fabricated evidence or misuse of the procedure may be addressed under applicable regulations. A grievance that is not upheld is not, by itself, evidence of bad faith.

16. ACCESSIBILITY

Students who require an accessible format, communication support or an adjustment to participate in the procedure should contact the Accessibility Support Office or Student Grievance Office. An approved adjustment does not change the standard used to decide the grievance.
