# Student ID Card Services

The Institute identity card is the primary identification document for students of Northbridge Institute of Technology. This guide explains how cards are issued, activated, replaced and returned.

## First issue of a student ID card

New students receive an ID card after admission, identity verification and completion of the required registration steps. The Student Services Centre will announce the collection schedule for each programme.

Students must collect their cards personally and present the original admission acknowledgement or another government-issued photo identity document. A card will not be released to a friend, class representative or family member.

The first card issued during admission is included in the student registration process and does not carry a separate card fee.

## Information printed on the card

The card normally shows the student's name, photograph, student number, programme, card issue date and validity period. Electronic records connected to the card may also be used for library access, attendance systems, examinations and approved campus facilities.

A student should check the printed details at the time of collection. A printing error reported within five working days will be corrected without a replacement charge.

## Card activation

Most new cards are activated before collection. Access to individual services may take up to one working day after the card is issued. If the card is recognised by one service but rejected by another, the student should report the affected service and location to the Student Services Centre.

Activation does not override a restriction placed by the Library, Finance and Accounts, Student Housing, the Controller of Examinations or another authorised office.

## Lost or stolen cards

A lost or stolen card must be reported immediately through the student portal. Reporting the card blocks its electronic use and creates a service reference number. Students should also report theft to campus security when the circumstances require it.

The Institute is not responsible for unauthorised use that occurs before the loss is reported. A card reported as lost must not be used if it is later found. The student should return it to the Student Services Centre for disposal.

## Replacement of a lost card

After reporting the loss, the student may submit an ID card replacement request through the portal. The request must include the service reference number and a recent photograph if the existing photograph no longer meets identification requirements.

The replacement charge is INR 500. Payment must be made through the Institute payment portal after the request is accepted. A payment receipt without an approved request does not authorise card production.

Replacement cards are normally ready within five working days after approval and confirmed payment. This period may be longer during admission, examination or mass-renewal activity.

## Damaged or malfunctioning cards

A damaged or unreadable card should be presented at the Student Services Centre. If the card has failed under ordinary use and shows no avoidable physical damage, it may be replaced without charge after inspection.

A replacement charge may apply when the card is cracked, deliberately altered, punched, cut, exposed to excessive heat or otherwise damaged through misuse. The old card must be surrendered before the replacement is issued.

## Changes to personal or academic details

The Student Services Centre cannot change a name, date of birth, programme or other official record only on the basis of a card request. The underlying student record must first be corrected by the authorised academic or admissions office.

After the approved change appears in the student information system, a revised card may be requested. No replacement charge applies when the reissue is required solely because of an approved Institute record correction or an Institute-directed programme change.

## Temporary identification letter

A student waiting for a replacement card may request a temporary identification letter. It is issued only after identity and current registration are verified and is normally valid for seven calendar days.

The letter does not automatically provide electronic door access, library borrowing or examination entry. The office responsible for each service decides whether additional verification is required. Examination identification remains subject to instructions issued by the Controller of Examinations.

## Proper use of the card

The student ID card is personal and must not be lent, copied, altered or used by another person. Students must present it when requested by an authorised Institute employee or security officer acting in an official capacity.

Possession of another student's card, use of an altered card or assistance in unauthorised access may result in withdrawal of the card and action under Institute regulations.

## Expiry and renewal

The validity date is printed on the card. Continuing students will receive renewal instructions when a physical replacement or electronic extension is required. A student must not modify an expiry date or continue using an expired card as proof of current registration.

## Graduation, withdrawal or termination of registration

The card remains Institute property. It must be returned when requested after graduation, withdrawal, cancellation of admission or termination of registration. Returning the card does not by itself complete academic, financial, library or hostel clearance.

## Contact and service hours

The Student Services Centre is open from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, except Institute holidays. Students may email `student.services@northbridge.example` or call extension 2102.

Requests concerning a specific card should include the student number and portal service reference. Full identity-document numbers should not be sent by ordinary email.
