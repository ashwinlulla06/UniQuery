# Library Services FAQ

This document explains the main services and rules of the Northbridge Institute of Technology Central Library. It applies from 1 August 2026 unless a later official notice changes a particular service.

## When is the library open?

During teaching weeks, the library is open from 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, and from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on Saturday. It is closed on Sundays and Institute holidays. Extended examination hours, when approved, are announced separately through official channels.

## Who can use the library?

Library membership is activated for registered students and current employees. A valid Institute ID card is required to enter controlled areas, borrow material and collect reserved items. Access may be suspended when a person's Institute registration or employment is inactive.

## How many books may a student borrow?

An undergraduate student may borrow up to five circulating books at a time. The standard loan period is 14 calendar days. A book may be renewed twice if it is not overdue, another user has not placed a hold on it and the item is not part of a restricted collection.

## How do holds and reservations work?

A student may place a hold on a circulating book through the library catalogue. When the book becomes available, the library sends a notice to the student's Institute email address. The item is held at the service desk for two working days and is then offered to the next person in the queue.

## What happens when a book is overdue?

The overdue charge is INR 5 per book per day for the first 14 overdue days and INR 10 per book per day after that. Borrowing privileges are suspended while an item remains overdue. A reminder is a courtesy; not receiving one does not cancel the return date or the charge.

## What should I do if a book is lost or damaged?

Report the loss or damage to the service desk promptly. The library may require an approved replacement copy of the same edition or payment of the assessed replacement cost, together with an INR 100 processing charge. Writing, highlighting, water damage, missing pages and damage to labels or security tags may be treated as damage. The library will confirm the settlement amount in writing.

## Can reference and course-reserve material be borrowed?

Reference works, archival material, theses and items marked "Library Use Only" must normally remain in the library. A course-reserve item may have a shorter loan period or overnight use when that permission is shown in the catalogue. The due time printed on the loan record applies.

## How can I use journals, databases and e-books?

Current students and employees may use licensed digital resources with their Institute credentials. Off-campus access is available through the library's remote-access portal where the licence permits it. Credentials must not be shared, and systematic downloading or redistribution of licensed content is prohibited.

## Are printing and scanning available?

Self-service printing and scanning are available during library hours. Current charges are displayed at the service desk and in the print portal. Users are responsible for complying with copyright and licence conditions when copying any material.

## Can study rooms be reserved?

Group-study rooms may be booked through the library portal by registered students. A booking may be released if the group does not arrive within 15 minutes. Quiet-study areas are not reservable and must be used without group discussion or calls.

## Does the library provide interlibrary loans?

Material not held by the Central Library may be requested through the interlibrary-loan service. A student may have up to three active requests. Lending-library restrictions apply, and any external copying, postage or replacement charge is passed to the requester after notice.

## What accessibility support is available?

The library provides step-free access, adjustable study spaces and assistance in locating or collecting material. Students who need an alternative format or another approved accommodation should contact the service desk or the Institute accessibility office as early as possible.

## What conduct is expected inside the library?

Users must follow the noise designation of each area. Food is not permitted near collections or computers; covered drinking water is allowed in designated areas. Personal belongings must not be used to reserve unattended seats. The Institute is not responsible for items left behind.

## What if my student ID card is lost?

Report the card through Student ID Card Services and inform the library. Borrowing on the missing card will be blocked. Temporary borrowing permission may be issued after identity and current registration are verified; a photograph or photocopy of the missing card is not accepted as a substitute.

## How do I obtain library clearance?

Students completing, withdrawing from or transferring out of a programme must return all borrowed material and settle confirmed charges. Clearance is recorded after the library account has been checked. Returning books to a department or another student does not complete library clearance.

## How can I contact the library?

Email `library.help@northbridge.example`, call extension 2204 or visit the Central Library service desk during opening hours. Include your student number and any catalogue, loan or request reference when asking about a specific transaction.

## Document authority

The Director of Library Services maintains this FAQ. If it conflicts with a later dated Institute regulation or official circular, the later authoritative document applies. Service-hour changes and temporary closures may be announced separately without issuing a new version of this FAQ.
